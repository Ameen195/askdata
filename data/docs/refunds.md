# Refunds and returns

Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days after delivery when an item is damaged, defective, or materially different from its listing. The customer should include the order number and photographs of the issue. Perishable, personalized, and clearly marked final-sale items are not eligible for change-of-mind returns, but this exclusion does not remove rights for damaged or incorrect goods.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method after the returned item is received and inspected. Processing usually takes 5 to 10 business days, depending on the payment provider. Return shipping is covered by the seller when the item is faulty or incorrect; change-of-mind return shipping is paid by the customer.
