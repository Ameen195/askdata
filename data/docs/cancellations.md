# Order cancellations

Customers can request cancellation before an order is marked dispatched. Once the carrier has received the parcel, the customer should follow the return process instead. A cancellation is not complete until the customer receives confirmation.

For a confirmed pre-dispatch cancellation, the full captured payment is refunded to the original payment method. Refund processing usually takes 5 to 10 business days. Sellers should not charge a cancellation fee for an order that has not been dispatched.
