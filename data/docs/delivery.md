# Delivery service levels

Orders are normally handed to the carrier within 2 business days after payment confirmation. The estimated delivery window is 3 to 7 business days after dispatch for domestic orders. Remote-area delivery can take up to 10 business days.

Tracking details are sent when the carrier scans the parcel. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, customers can contact support with the order number. A parcel more than 10 business days past its estimated delivery date is escalated for a carrier investigation. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.
