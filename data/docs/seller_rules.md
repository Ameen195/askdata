# Seller listing and service rules

Sellers must keep stock counts, prices, product descriptions, and photographs accurate. Listings must identify the product condition, disclose known defects, and avoid unsupported safety, health, or performance claims. Sellers may not list counterfeit, recalled, prohibited, or stolen goods.

Sellers should acknowledge customer messages within 2 business days and provide dispatch confirmation and valid tracking promptly. Repeated late dispatch, misleading listings, or unresolved customer complaints may lead to listing removal or account review. Sellers must follow applicable consumer protection and product safety laws.
